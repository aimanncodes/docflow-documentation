## Common problems

:::details I did not receive the invitation email
Check your junk folder. Ask your administrator to resend it from your user record with **Send Welcome Email**, and to check that outgoing email is set up.
:::

:::details I lost my authenticator phone
Your administrator can reset two-factor authentication for your user. You will set it up again at your next sign-in.
:::
