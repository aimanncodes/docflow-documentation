## Setting up the website and portal

1. Set your logo, navigation and footer in [website settings](/website/web-pages).
2. Choose or create a [theme](/website/themes-branding).
3. Publish the main pages in English and [Arabic](/website/arabic-website).
4. Add an enquiry [web form](/website/web-forms).
5. Invite key customers and suppliers to the [portal](/website/portal).

## Common problems

:::details Submissions are not arriving
Check that the form is published, that **Login Required** matches your intent, and that the document type's permissions allow guests to create records if login is not required.
:::
