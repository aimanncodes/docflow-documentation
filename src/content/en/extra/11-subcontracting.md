## When to subcontract

| Reason | Example |
| --- | --- |
| You lack the equipment | Hot-dip galvanising, CNC machining |
| Capacity is full | Overflow welding during a large project |
| A specialist is cheaper | Private-label packing, printing |
| Regulation requires a licensed party | Certain chemical treatments |

## Behind the scenes

On receipt, raw materials worth AED 46,000 are consumed from the supplier warehouse, the service cost of AED 9,600 is credited to **Stock Received But Not Billed**, and finished goods are debited at AED 55,600 plus any additional costs.

## Common problems

:::details "Not enough raw material at supplier warehouse"
More finished goods were received than the materials sent can cover. Transfer the missing materials, or correct the consumed quantities.
:::
