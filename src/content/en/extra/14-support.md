## Key fields

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Raised By | The email or contact of the person who reported it |
| Customer | Links the issue to the customer and their SLA |
| Issue Type and Priority | Classification and urgency |
| Service Level Agreement | Applied automatically from customer and priority |
| Response By / Resolution By | Deadlines from the SLA |
| Resolution Details | What was done, for the record |
