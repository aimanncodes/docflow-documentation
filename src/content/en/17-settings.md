@@ overview | Settings at a glance
@summary The Settings tile holds Global Defaults, System Settings and one settings page for each business module. This page lists them and links to the detailed pages.

The **Settings** tile on the home screen holds the settings that apply across the system. Each page sets the rules and defaults for one area. Most need the **System Manager** role, or the manager role of that module.

**Where to find it:** the **Settings** tile on the home screen. The pages are listed in the sidebar.

!fig The Settings sidebar with Global Defaults, System Settings, Accounts Settings, POS Settings, Selling Settings, Buying Settings, Stock Settings, Manufacturing Settings, Projects Settings, CRM Settings, Support Settings and Other Settings, with System Settings open @shot 09-administration/02-system-settings.png

## The settings pages

| Page | What it controls | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Global Defaults** | The defaults the whole system starts from | |
| **System Settings** | Country, language, time zone, date and number formats, passwords, sessions and backups | [System Settings and the country](/administration/system-settings) |
| **Accounts Settings** | Company-wide accounting rules for invoicing, payments, credit control and period locking | [Accounts settings](/accounting/accounts-settings) |
| **POS Settings** | The point of sale | [Point of sale](/accounting/pos) |
| **Selling Settings** | Customer naming, defaults, required documents and pricing in the sales process | [Selling settings](/selling/selling-settings) |
| **Buying Settings** | Supplier naming, required documents and rate behaviour in purchasing | [Buying settings](/buying/buying-settings) |
| **Stock Settings** | Valuation, negative stock, batches and serial numbers, over-delivery and reservation | [Stock settings](/stock/stock-settings) |
| **Manufacturing Settings** | Default warehouses, backflush, overproduction and capacity planning | [Manufacturing settings](/manufacturing/manufacturing-settings) |
| **Projects Settings** | Timesheet rules and overlap checks | [Project settings](/projects/project-settings) |
| **CRM Settings** | Lead and opportunity defaults and duplicate checks | [CRM settings](/crm/crm-settings) |
| **Support Settings** | Issue auto-close, the support portal and service levels | [Support settings](/support/support-settings) |

## Settings kept in their own modules

The settings of some DocFlow modules are not in this tile. You find them in the module itself:

- **VAT Settings**: see [Setting up VAT](/vat-compliance/vat-setup).
- The HR and payroll settings: see [HR and payroll settings](/human-resources/hr-settings).

## Before you change a setting

- Read the detailed page first. Many settings change how documents are checked or posted from the moment you save.
- Change one thing at a time, and try it on a test document.
- Write down what you changed and why.
